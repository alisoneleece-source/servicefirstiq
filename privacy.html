This notice describes how information provided through Service First IQ website enquiries is used.

Information you choose to provide

The enquiry form asks for your name, email, the service you are interested in and your message. Organization and phone number are optional. Please do not include sensitive personal information.

Purpose of collection

Enquiry details are used to respond to your request and discuss relevant services. Sending an enquiry does not subscribe you to marketing communications.

Form delivery and service providers

We use Formspree to process and store form submissions and deliver enquiry notifications to our configured email address. When you submit this form, the details you enter are sent to Formspree. See Formspree’s privacy policy for information about its data handling. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages.

Retention, access and questions

You can reply to our response to ask about your enquiry information. The business contact details and retention schedule are still being finalized; this notice will be updated when they are confirmed.
